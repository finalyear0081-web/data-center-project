Neural Network Specifications
Architecture:
Stacked LSTM (64 units) → Dropout(0.1) → LSTM (64 units) → Dropout(0.1) → Dense(64) → Dense(144 outputs for 10-min HazardNet / 288 for 5-min).
Optimizer & Loss:
Adam Optimizer ($\alpha=0.001$), Thermal-Weighted Loss with Peak Multiplier, EarlyStopping ($patience=4$).
Data Leakage Prevention:
Chronological 70% Train, 15% Val, 15% Test. MinMax scalers fitted solely on the training partition.
